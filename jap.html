About Japa

Japa (जप) is the meditative repetition of a mantra. It is a core practice in Hindu, Buddhist, and Sikh traditions for focusing the mind and connecting with the divine.

Mala beads typically have 108 beads — a sacred number in Eastern traditions representing the wholeness of existence. One full mala = 108 recitations.

How to practice:

  • Sit comfortably with a calm mind and set your intention
  • Recite the mantra aloud, in a whisper, or silently
  • Use the counter to track — one tap per recitation
  • Focus on the sound and meaning of the mantra
  • Consistency matters more than quantity — daily practice is key

Each mantra carries a specific vibration and intention. Choose the one that resonates with your current need.
