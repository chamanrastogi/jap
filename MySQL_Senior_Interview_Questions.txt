MySQL Interview Questions for Senior PHP Developers (11+ Years)

SQL Fundamentals

1.  What is the difference between SQL and MySQL?
2.  What are DDL, DML, DCL, TCL, and DQL?
3.  Explain PRIMARY KEY, FOREIGN KEY, UNIQUE KEY, and CHECK constraints.
