At-Home Exercise & Yoga Guide

Simple poses and bodyweight exercises you can do at home with zero equipment. Follow along daily for best results.

Morning Yoga Flow

Downward Dog

Full body stretch â€” hamstrings, shoulders, spine

  1. Start on hands and knees, tuck toes under
  2. Lift hips up and back, straighten legs
  3. Press heels gently toward floor, hold 5 deep breaths
  4. Pedal your feet to loosen hamstrings
Tip: Keep a slight bend in knees if hamstrings feel tight
Cat-Cow Stretch

Spine mobility â€” relieves back stiffness

  1. Start on hands and knees, spine neutral
  2. Cat: Round spine up, tuck chin, engage core
  3. Cow: Drop belly, lift chest and tailbone
  4. Alternate slowly with breath â€” 10 rounds
Tip: Move with your breath â€” inhale cow, exhale cat
Child's Pose

Restorative pose â€” relaxes lower back and mind

  1. Kneel on floor, touch big toes together
  2. Sit back on heels, extend arms forward
  3. Rest forehead on mat, breathe deeply for 60s
  4. Walk fingertips to one side to stretch obliques
Tip: Place a pillow under thighs if knees feel pressure

Bodyweight Circuit

Squats

Legs, glutes, core â€” the king of lower body moves

  1. Stand feet shoulder-width apart, toes slightly out
  2. Sit back as if sitting in a chair, keep chest up
  3. Go down until thighs parallel to floor
  4. Press through heels to stand up â€” 3 sets of 12
Tip: Keep knees tracking over toes, not collapsing inward
Plank

Core, shoulders, stability â€” total body tension

  1. Start in push-up position, hands under shoulders
  2. Engage glutes and core, body in a straight line
  3. Hold without dropping hips â€” 3 sets of 20-30s
  4. Progress to side plank for obliques
Tip: Drop to forearms if wrists feel strained
Walking Lunges

Legs, glutes, balance â€” functional movement

  1. Stand tall, step forward with right leg
  2. Lower until both knees are at 90 degrees
  3. Front knee over ankle, back knee hovering
  4. Push off back foot and step forward â€” 3 sets of 10 each leg
Tip: Keep torso upright, don't lean forward

Home Workout Tips

Start Slow, Build Consistency

Begin with 15 min/day, 4 days a week. Add 5 min each week. Consistency beats intensity â€” a short daily workout beats a long weekly one.

Form Over Everything

Quality reps > quantity. Do exercises in front of a mirror to check alignment. Bad form = wasted effort + injury risk.

Music & Tracking Keep You Going

Create a high-energy playlist. Track sets/reps in a notebook or phone. Seeing progress over weeks is the best motivation.

Always Cool Down

Finish every session with 5 min of deep stretching â€” hold each stretch 20-30s. Improves flexibility, reduces soreness.

Quick Relaxation â€” 4-7-8 Breathing

Close your eyes. Inhale through nose for 4 counts. Hold breath for 7 counts. Exhale slowly through mouth for 8 counts. Repeat 4 times. Lowers stress, improves digestion, and supports weight loss.
