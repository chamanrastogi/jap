Indian Diet Chart

40-Year-Old Working Man — Weight Loss Plan ~1,850 kcal High Protein

A sustainable 7-day Indian diet plan for belly fat reduction and weight loss (75 kg → 65–68 kg) with high protein, low sugar, and easy-to-cook meals for a sedentary office worker.

Protein Target
90–100 g/day

Daily Non-Negotiables

Hydration
  • 3–4 litres of water daily
  • 2 glasses right after waking up
  • 1 glass before every meal
  • Carry a 1L bottle to office
Movement
  • 10,000 steps/day (track with phone)
  • 30-min brisk walk in morning
  • 15–20 min walk after dinner
  • Take stairs, walk while on calls
Sleep
  • 7–8 hours every night
  • 11 PM – 7 AM schedule
  • No phone 30 min before bed
  • Consistent timing = better metabolism
Cheat Meal
  • 1 small cheat meal per week
  • Saturday lunch or Sunday
  • Choose one indulgence — not a full binge
  • Get back on track immediately next meal

Meal Prep Tips for Office

Sunday Prep
  • Chop veggies for the week, store in airtight boxes
  • Boil eggs, roast chana/makhana, portion nuts into 20g packets
  • Cook and refrigerate dal for 2–3 days
  • Prepare roti dough; it stays fresh in fridge for 2 days
Office Carry Kit
  • Pack of roasted chana / makhana
  • Green tea bags + your own cup
  • 1L water bottle (refill twice)
  • Cut cucumber / carrot sticks for sudden hunger
Night Rituals
  • Soak almonds + methi seeds for next morning
  • Pack lunch tiffin right after dinner
  • Keep water bottle by bedside
Smart Hacks
  • Never grocery shop on an empty stomach
  • Use smaller plates — portion control is visual
  • Drink a full glass of water 20 min before meals
  • Brush teeth right after dinner to avoid late snacking
