About switchwords

Switchwords are single words discovered by James T. Mangan in the 1960s that work directly with your subconscious mind to manifest desired outcomes.

Think of them as linguistic shortcuts — each word carries a specific vibrational energy. When you repeat a switchword, it bypasses your conscious mind (the part that overthinks) and reprograms your subconscious, where 95% of your thoughts and behaviors live.

How to use:

  • Choose a word or combination matching your intention
  • Repeat it aloud, silently, or in writing — consistent repetition is key
  • Visualize the desired outcome while repeating
  • No special belief required — just repetition creates the shift

Master switchword: TOGETHER — aligns your conscious and subconscious as one. It's the most versatile and powerful word.

Tip: Combine 2-4 words for even stronger results. Try the Prescriptions section for ready-made combos.

Switchwords were developed by James T. Mangan (1896–1970), author of The Secret of Perfect Living. These words are used in manifestation communities worldwide as spiritual focus tools and affirmation-style chants.
